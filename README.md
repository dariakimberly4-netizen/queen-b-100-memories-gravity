# 👑 Queen B — 100 Memories vs Gravity

An interactive cinematic birthday experience built around **100 real memories and one final video**.

## ✨ The Experience

Queen B stands beside a mysterious glowing **B**. When the visitor taps **BREAK GRAVITY**, gravity disappears and 100 real photos burst into a floating memory universe.

Visitors can drag with one finger on mobile or click-and-drag on desktop to explore the memories. Each photo can be opened individually while preserving its original image and proportions.

After a memory is viewed, it releases a glowing butterfly that flies toward the B.

**MEMORIES AWAKENED — 0 / 100**

The world evolves as memories are discovered:

- **25 / 100** — stars begin appearing
- **50 / 100** — the B starts glowing from within
- **75 / 100** — butterflies begin forming a crown around Queen B
- **99 / 100** — the universe becomes almost completely still
- **100 / 100** — the final butterfly lands and the B erupts in golden light

The particles then transform into a cinematic screen for the single emotional finale video.

## 🎬 Finale

> You thought these were 100 memories.
>
> They were 100 pieces of you.
>
> **ONE LAST MEMORY.**

The finale video plays, then the 100 photos return and form a giant glowing **B constellation** behind Queen B.

### BEAUX — THE QUEEN B
**100 memories. One extraordinary life.**

## 🎮 Interaction

- Mobile-first experience
- One-finger drag navigation
- Optional device tilt exploration where supported
- Desktop click-and-drag support
- Tap a photo to view it
- Return to the exact memory universe after closing a photo
- No photo repeats until all 100 memories are represented
- Original photo orientation and aspect ratio preserved
- Reduced-motion friendly fallback
- Large, accessible touch targets

## 🎨 Visual Direction

Deep royal purple, midnight blue, restrained gold light, magical particles, butterflies, cinematic depth and an elegant Queen B fantasy atmosphere.

This is **not** a slideshow, conventional gallery, collage, dashboard, or photo grid. The memories exist as objects inside one continuous interactive world.
